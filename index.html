What it actually does

Not a wrapper around a spreadsheet. Everything below reads straight off your own combat log.

Live combat meter

Damage share, totals, and DPS per ally — each on their own clock, started at their own first action, so a late joiner's number is honest. Adds fold into the engagement instead of splitting it.

Partial-resist detection

Rolling per-spell landing averages catch a spell being shredded within a handful of casts — judged against your recent zones under the same invocation, with a pointer to what's still landing.

Never re-enter your class

Detected automatically from what you actually cast — updates the moment your loadout changes, mid-session.

Drop Watch

A heads-up the moment you're fighting something that drops what you're after — then tells you exactly where it landed. Tier variants fold to the base item automatically.

Sky Quests & class unlocks

Every remaining quest's materials in one list, demand-aware — an item needed by three quests gets one entry with real remaining count. Updates live as loot hits your bags.

Maps with real GPS

Routes computed over the server's own navigation meshes — around geometry, not through it — including hops through teleporter pads and portal rings. Browse a zone's NPCs, click one, path to it.

Character, gear & planner

Level estimates, AA history, gear recommendations that understand tiers and exalts, and a planner that keeps your own edits marked and persistent.

Inventory, bags to bank

Browse every bag, bank slot, and depot item — and jump straight to where a specific item actually is.

An overlay that stays out of the way

DPS meter, skill cooldowns, target effects, CC status, drop alerts — each its own click-through window with its own opacity, re-asserting itself over borderless fullscreen.

Runs on your machine, full stop

No account, no server, no telemetry. It reads a text file on disk and shows you what's in it — that's the whole trust model.

Under the hood

The short version. The long version is in the repo.

eqlog_*.txt→tail @ 100ms→frame→classify→typed action→ingest→columnar store→query→UI

tail

The newest eqlog_*.txt is polled every 100 ms by byte offset and file identity. Rotation, truncation, and replacement are all handled; the log is only ever opened read-only.

classify

Two stages: a single multi-pattern anchor pass narrows each line to candidate rules, then per-rule regex extracts fields. The result is one of ~160 typed actions — every line accounted for, matched or not.

store

Append-only columnar event store with interned names; encounters are row ranges over it, not copies. A full multi-month raid log replays in seconds inside a few hundred MB.

encounters

A streaming fight graph decides boundaries: idle windows close fights, mezz keeps them alive, resets link to re-engagements, and a corpse from a merged pull is folded into its keeper. Kill and reset are distinct outcomes, never guessed.

replay

Deterministic: the same log always produces the same state. History backfills in parallel across cores, then live lines apply in order — restart the app and it rebuilds everything from the file alone.

privacy

No account, no telemetry, no upload. The only network calls are checking for app updates and fetching public map data — your log never leaves the machine.
